# 纹像（TextureStudio）隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 28 日

纹像尊重并保护你的隐私。本隐私政策说明纹像如何处理信息。

## 数据收集

纹像不会收集、存储、上传或出售任何个人数据，也不会使用广告、分析、追踪或第三方数据收集 SDK。App 不要求注册或登录账号。

## 照片与图像处理

只有在你主动选择照片时，纹像才会访问你选择的图像。所有滤镜、纹理效果和图像处理均在你的设备本地完成，原始照片和处理结果不会上传到开发者服务器。

当你选择保存结果时，App 会请求向系统照片图库添加图片的权限。使用系统分享菜单时，数据只会发送到你主动选择的 App 或服务；这些第三方的隐私处理方式受其各自政策约束。

## 数据共享与保留

纹像不收集数据，因此不会与开发者或第三方共享或保留用户数据。

## 儿童隐私

纹像不会有意收集儿童或其他用户的个人信息。

## 政策更新

如果 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策将相应更新，并在本页面标明新的生效日期。

## 联系方式

如对本隐私政策有任何问题，请访问 [https://charlo.cn/](https://charlo.cn/)。

# Privacy Policy for 纹像 (TextureStudio)

Effective date: August 28, 2026

纹像 respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

## Data Collection

纹像 does not collect, store, upload, or sell personal data. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs, and it does not require account registration or login.

## Photos and Image Processing

纹像 accesses an image only when you choose it. All filters, texture effects, and image processing are performed locally on your device. Original photos and processed results are never uploaded to a developer-operated server.

When you save a result, the app requests permission to add the image to your system photo library. If you use the system share sheet, data is sent only to the app or service you select. Those third parties handle data under their own privacy policies.

## Data Sharing and Retention

Because 纹像 does not collect user data, it does not share or retain user data.

## Children's Privacy

纹像 does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app's data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated and a new effective date will be shown on this page.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, visit [https://charlo.cn/](https://charlo.cn/).
